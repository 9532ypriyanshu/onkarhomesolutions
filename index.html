Ideal for Residential Homes

On-Grid Solar Systems (Net Metering)

On-Grid (or Grid-Tied) systems are the most popular and cost-effective solar installations for Lucknow households with regular DISCOM electricity supply.

Bi-Directional Net Metering: Excess power generated during sunny daylight is fed back to the UPPCL grid. Your meter rotates backwards, wiping out your billing units!
Full PM Surya Ghar Subsidy: 100% eligible for the central DBT subsidy up to ₹78,000.
No Costly Battery Replacement: Lowest maintenance cost since there is no chemical battery depreciation.
Uninterrupted 24x7 Power

Hybrid Solar Systems (Grid + Battery)

Designed specifically for areas that experience intermittent power fluctuations or prolonged power cuts. A hybrid system combines grid export capabilities with an intelligent energy storage battery bank.

Continuous Power During Blackouts: When the grid fails, your critical loads (fans, lights, refrigerators, Wi-Fi, televisions) remain powered instantly.
Lithium or Solar C10 Tubular: Compatible with both advanced high-density Lithium Ferro Phosphate (LFP) batteries and economical tubular batteries.
Dual Operating Modes: Switch between self-consumption priority or maximum grid-feed mode with smartphone monitoring.
